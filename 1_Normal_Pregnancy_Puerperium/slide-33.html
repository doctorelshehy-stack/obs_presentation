till delivery

Initial Visit

  • Diagnosis of pregnancy & accurate dating
  • Complete clinical evaluation: history, general, abdominal, local examination

Subsequent Visits

  • Ask about any complaints
  • BP, weight, edema, abdominal examination
  • Local examination (if indicated)
  • Urine for albumin, sugar, other investigations
