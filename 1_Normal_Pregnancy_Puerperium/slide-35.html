Antenatal Advices

Nutrition

Total calories: 2700 cal/day
Protein: 1.5 gm/kg/day (≥½ animal source)
Iron: 30-60 mg/day
Calcium: 1.5 gm/day
Folic acid: 400 µg/day
Weight gain: 10-12 kg

General Advices

  • Teeth: Daily cleaning by toothpaste
  • Clothing: Light clothes, avoid high heels
  • Bathing: Avoid hot bathing & swimming pools
  • Sexual activity: Avoid if threat of abortion/PTL; otherwise allowed with less frequency
  • Sleep: 8 hrs night + 2 hrs afternoon
  • Travelling: Avoid if risk of abortion or PTL
  • Special habits: Avoid smoking, alcohol
