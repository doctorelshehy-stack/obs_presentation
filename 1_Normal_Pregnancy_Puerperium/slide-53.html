Management of Normal Puerperium

Immediate Postpartum Care

  • Vital signs: Frequent observation
  • Uterus: Massage + monitoring vaginal bleeding every 15 min for ≥1 hr
  • Position: Semi-sitting → encourages lochia drainage
  • Ambulation: Movement + breathing exercises → minimize DVT risk
  • Diet: 2 hrs after uncomplicated vaginal delivery, no dietary restriction

Ongoing Care

  • Perineal care: Wash with antiseptic lotion. Add local antibiotic if stitches
  • Breast care: Before each feed, wash nipple & areola with warm water & soap
  • Bowel care: Green vegetables & fruits to avoid constipation
  • Newborn care: If mother Rh-ve & baby Rh+ve → anti-D within 1st 72 hrs
  • Pelvic floor exercise: Alternating contraction & relaxation
  • Future contraception: Counsel mother before leaving hospital
