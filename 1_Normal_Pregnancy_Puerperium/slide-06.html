Formation of Chorionic Villi

Primary Villus

  • Trophoblast differentiates into:
  • Cytotrophoblast (inner layer)
  • Syncytiotrophoblast (outer layer)
  • Trophoblast forms finger-like projections into decidua

Secondary Villus

  • Mesoderm develops in the primary villus
  • Forms connective tissue (CT) core
  • Becomes the 2ry villus

Tertiary Villus

  • Vascularization of 2ry villus with fetal vessels
  • Forms the functional 3ry villus
  • Anchoring villi: Fixation & attachment
  • Free villi: Food & nutrition (highly vascular)
  • Functional unit: HCG → maintain CL for 10 wks
