Fetal Skull — Bones, Sutures & Fontanelles

Parts & Bones

  • 3 parts: Base (chin→foramen magnum), Face (chin→root of nose), Vault (3 regions)
  • 2 frontal bones — separated by frontal suture
  • 2 parietal bones — separated by sagittal suture
  • Occipital bone — separated by lambdoid suture
  • Coronal suture: frontal from parietal
  • Temporal suture: parietal from temporal
  • Vertex: bounded by ant. fontanelle + coronal suture, post. fontanelle + lambdoid, lines through parietal eminences
  • Brow: from nose/supra-orbital ridges to ant. fontanelle + coronal suture

Fontanelles

6 areas at meeting of sutures. 4 temporal (no obstetric importance).
Anterior (Bregma) & Posterior (Lambda) important to diagnose:
• Vertex presentation · Position of occiput · Degree of head flexion
