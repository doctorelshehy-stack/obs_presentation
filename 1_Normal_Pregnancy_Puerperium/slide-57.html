Fetal Movement Counting & NST

Fetal Movement Counting

  • Timing: After 30 weeks' gestation
  • Method: Mother counts kicks in 3 hrs during 12-hr period (9am-9pm) — 1 hr morning, 1 hr midday, 1 hr evening
  • Multiply by 4 to get kicks/12 hrs
  • If <10 kicks → needs Non-Stress Test (NST)

Non-Stress Test (NST)

  • Purpose: FHR acceleration in response to fetal movement (sign of fetal health)
  • Timing: After 32 weeks' gestation
  • Method: Doppler-detected FHR acceleration coincident with fetal movements perceived by mother
  • Normal (reactive): ≥2 accelerations ≥15 bpm above baseline, lasting ≥15 sec, within 20 min
  • Abnormal: Baseline oscillates <5 bpm
  • Frequency: Every 7 days. More frequent for post-term, DM type 1, FGR, hypertension
