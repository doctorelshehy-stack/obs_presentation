Choriodecidual Spaces & Placenta Structure

Choriodecidual Spaces

  • Trophoblast invades endometrial vessels → delivers maternal blood into chorio-decidual space → 1st wave of invasion
  • At 20 weeks, trophoblast invades & destroys media of spiral vessels → wide mouthed sinuses → 2nd wave of invasion
  • The rest of chorion not sharing → Chorion leave
  • Decidua overlying ovum → Decidua capsularis
  • Decidua covering rest of cavity → Decidua parietalis (vera)

Placenta Structure

  • Shape: Discoid, 500 gm, 20-25 cm diameter
  • Thickness: 2 cm center, thins towards periphery
  • Cord insertion: Eccentric
  • 2 Surfaces: Fetal (smooth, covered by membranes) & Maternal (divided into 12-15 cotyledons)
  • 2 Parts: Fetal → Chorion frondosum, Maternal → Decidua basalis
