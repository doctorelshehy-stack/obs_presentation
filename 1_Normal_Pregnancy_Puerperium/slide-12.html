Placental Hormones — Proteins

Human Chorionic Gonadotrophin (HCG)

Source: Syncytiotrophoblast

Detected one week before missed period

2 subunits: α (similar to FSH, LH, TSH) and β (specific to hCG)

Function

  • Maintenance of CL (luteotropic)
  • Immunological suppressive action
  • Stimulates fetal testosterone

Uses

  • Diagnosis of pregnancy
  • Diagnosis of abnormalities (↑ in Down)
  • Follow up of vesicular mole

Human Placental Lactogen (HPL)

Source: Syncytiotrophoblast

Very similar to GH & prolactin

Functions:

  • May stimulate growth of breasts
  • On CHO & fat (anti-insulin effect) → spares glucose & fatty acids for fetus
