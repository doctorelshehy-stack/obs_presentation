Clinical Correlation & Student Activity

Clinical Correlation

Striae gravidarum — pink-purple abdominal stretch marks due to cortisol, estrogen, relaxin
Chloasma gravidarum — facial hyperpigmentation patches due to MSH, estrogen, progesterone
Montgomery tubercles — enlarged areolar sebaceous glands (estrogen, progesterone)
