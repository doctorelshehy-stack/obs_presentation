Gastrointestinal Tract Changes

Mouth & Appetite

  • Gingivitis: Increased vascularity & hypertrophy of interdental papillae
  • Ptyalism: Increased salivation (increased vascularity of salivary glands)
  • Appetite changes (longing): Likes certain foods
  • Reduced taste buds → desire for markedly sweet, sour, salt foods

Nausea & Heart Burn

  • Nausea (morning sickness) & vomiting (emesis gravidarum): Starts 4th-6th weeks, improves by ~12th weeks
  • Heart burn: Reflux of acidic gastric contents → esophagus (relaxing effect of progesterone on esophageal sphincter)

Intestinal & Other Changes

  • Indigestion & flatulence: Reduced gastric motility + reflux of alkaline intestinal contents (progesterone)
  • Constipation: Reduced large intestinal motility (progesterone)
  • Piles: Laxity of vein walls (progesterone)
  • Appendix: Displaced upward and laterally
  • Gall bladder: More tendency for stones (delayed emptying)
  • Liver: ↓ albumin, ↑ globulin (↓ A/G ratio), ↑ alkaline phosphatase
