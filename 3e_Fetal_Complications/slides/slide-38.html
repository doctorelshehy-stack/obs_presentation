Summary

✦ Amniotic Fluid Abnormalities – Polyhydramnios (AFV >2000 mL) & Oligohydramnios (AFV <500 mL)
✦ IUGR – Fetal growth <10th centile; asymmetric (placental) vs symmetric (genetic)
✦ Postterm Pregnancy – Beyond 42 weeks; risks of placental insufficiency & macrosomia
✦ Macrosomia – Fetal weight >4000 g; risk of shoulder dystocia & birth injuries
✦ IUFD – Fetal death after 20 weeks; manage with termination & monitoring for DIC
✦ Fetal Birth Injuries – Fractures, nerve palsies, cephalohematomas, lacerations
