Amniotic Fluid Abnormalities

ILOs:

  • Understand the dynamics of amniotic fluid
  • Describe the difference between polyhydramnios and oligohydramnios
  • Explain the management of either excess or poor amount of amniotic fluid

Production & Resorption of Amniotic Fluid:

• During first half of pregnancy: from maternal circulation

• During second half of pregnancy: fetal urination

• Resorption: through fetal swallowing

Function of Amniotic Fluid:

• Surrounds the growing fetus and protects from injury and temperature changes

• Allows for fetal movement and permits musculoskeletal development
