IUFD – Fetal & Placental Causes

Fetal Causes

• Intrauterine growth restriction (IUGR)

• Congenital abnormality

• Genetic abnormality

• Infection (Parvovirus B19, CMV, Listeria)

• Hydrops

Placental Causes

• Cord accident

• Abruption

• Premature rupture of membranes

• Vasa previa

• Feto-maternal hemorrhage

• Placental insufficiency
