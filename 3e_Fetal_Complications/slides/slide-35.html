Nerve Palsies

Erb's palsy and facial palsy are the most common nerve palsies.

Erb's Palsy

Cause: Damage to the brachial plexus (C5, 6 and 7)

Association: Traction on the neck and shoulders during difficult deliveries

Presentation: Flaccid arm held in a pronated and internally rotated position

Treatment: Early physiotherapy; surgical treatment techniques to repair damaged nerves are now available

Facial Palsy

Cause: Obstetric maneuvers, such as the use of forceps causing pressure damage

Mechanism: External pressure causing a lower motor neuron injury

Prognosis: Excellent

Note: If an upper motor neuron lesion is suspected, investigate for possible cerebral injury or congenital disorders. No specific treatment required.
