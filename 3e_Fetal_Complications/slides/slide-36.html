Sternomastoid Tumors & Lacerations

Sternomastoid Tumors

• Result of bleeding into the sternomastoid muscle

• Not normally recognized at birth; become obvious a few weeks of age

• Physiotherapy is required to prevent contracture of the muscle

• Prognosis is good

Lacerations

• The infant may suffer skin lacerations during delivery, usually during CS

• Usually superficial and heal without problems

• Suturing may be needed for deeper wounds
