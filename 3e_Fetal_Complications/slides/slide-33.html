Fractures

Fractures usually result from traumatic deliveries as in shoulder dystocia and difficult instrumental deliveries.

Clavicular Fracture

• Most frequently seen fracture

• Best treated conservatively

• Mild analgesia may be helpful

Long Bone Fractures

• Humerus and femur

• May require simple splinting to immobilise the limb and reduce pain

Skull Fractures

• More serious

• Possibility of underlying hemorrhage must be considered

Prognosis

The majority of neonatal fractures will heal with conservative treatment.
