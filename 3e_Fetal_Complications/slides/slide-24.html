Macrosomia – Definition & Risk Factors

ILOs:

  • Understand the definition of macrosomia
  • Explain the risk factors of macrosomia
  • Describe the management and complications of fetal macrosomia

Definition

A fetal weight more than 4,000 grams.

Risk Factors

• History of fetal macrosomia

• Maternal obesity

• Excessive weight gain during pregnancy

• Previous pregnancies (risk increases with each)

• Male infants (typically weigh slightly more)

• Maternal age > 35 years

Causes

1. Genetic factors

2. Maternal conditions (e.g., diabetes)

3. Multiparity

4. Postterm pregnancy
