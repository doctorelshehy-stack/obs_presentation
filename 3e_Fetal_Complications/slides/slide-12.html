Types of IUGR

Asymmetric IUGR

Characteristic: Disproportionate growth restriction with greater decrease in fetal body and limbs compared to head circumference.

Cause: Extrinsic factors such as placental insufficiency.

Mechanism: Oxygen and nutrients are directed towards vital organs (brain and heart) by passing other organs (e.g., liver, muscle, and fat tissue).

Timing: Affects the fetus later in gestation.

Symmetric IUGR

Characteristic: Proportional growth restriction in all parts of the fetus.

Cause: Intrinsic factors such as genetic abnormalities and intrauterine infections.

Mechanism: Early insult affects all cells equally.

Timing: Affects the fetus early in gestation.
