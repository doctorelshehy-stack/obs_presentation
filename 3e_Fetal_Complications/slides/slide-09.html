Management

Polyhydramnios

• Amnio-reduction – therapeutic drainage of excess fluid

• Prostaglandin synthetase inhibitor (e.g., Indomethacin)

• Treatment of the cause e.g., control of diabetes mellitus

Oligohydramnios

• Amnio-infusion – instillation of fluid into the amniotic cavity

• Rehydration of the mother – oral or IV fluids

• Postterm pregnancy: termination of pregnancy

📋 Student Activity

Each student is requested to attend ultrasonographic obstetric evaluation of a third trimester pregnant woman at departmental outpatient clinic to check with the sonographer how amniotic fluid is evaluated.
