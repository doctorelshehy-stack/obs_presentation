Macrosomia – Picture & Management

Clinical Picture

• Large fundal height

• Excessive amniotic fluid (polyhydramnios)

Complications

• Prolonged and obstructed labor

• Shoulder dystocia

• Fetal injuries

• Meconium aspiration syndrome

• Genital tract lacerations

• Postpartum hemorrhage

• Uterine rupture

Treatment

1. Proper antenatal care: to prevent macrosomia and diagnose it before labor.

2. Cesarean section: is safe for both mother and fetus.

3. Postnatal: proper examination of the neonate and management of any problem.

📋 Student Activity

Each student is requested to attend the weighing of a newborn to assign whether it is macrosomic or not. In case of newborn diagnosed to be macrosomic, the etiology should be sought with the aid of the tutor.
