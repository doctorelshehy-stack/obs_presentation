Cephalohaematoma vs Caput Succedaneum

FeatureCephalohaematomaCaput Succedaneum
OnsetDevelops hours or days after birthPresent at birth
LocationLocalized hematoma to one bone, limited by suturesDiffuse tissue oedema overlying more than one bone
EdgesWell-defined edgesIll-defined edges
ConsistencyElastic (does not pit on pressure)Soft (pits on pressure)
ResolutionDisappears within few weeksDisappears within 1-2 days
