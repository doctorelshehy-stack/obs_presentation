Investigations

• 75 g oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT)

• Leukocytic count in cases of PROM

Ultrasound Assessment

Single Deepest Pocket (SDP)

• SDP < 2 cm = oligohydramnios

• SDP between 2 – 8 cm = Normal

• SDP > 8 cm = polyhydramnios

Amniotic Fluid Index (AFI)

The deepest amniotic pocket in each of the four quadrants is measured vertically and the values added together (The 4-quadrant method).

• AFI < 5 cm = oligohydramnios

• AFI between 10 and 20 cm = normal

• AFI > 25 cm = polyhydramnios
