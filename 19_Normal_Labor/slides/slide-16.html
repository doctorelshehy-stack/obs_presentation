Mechanism of Normal Labor

A. Delivery of the Head

1. Descent
Caused by uterine contractions
2. Engagement
Biparietal diameter through pelvic brim
3. Flexion
Increased flexion of head
4. Internal Rotation
Rotation of presenting part
5. Extension
Extension through birth canal
6. Restitution
Return to normal alignment
7. External Rotation
Completion of rotation

Engagement diagnosed by:

  • Abdominally: Rule of fifths — 2/5 or less of the head is felt abdominally above the symphysis pubis by the pelvic grip.
  • Vaginally: The vertex is felt at or below the ischial spine (zero station).
B. Delivery of the Shoulder and Body
Following delivery of the head, the shoulders and body are delivered through similar mechanism of cardinal movements.
