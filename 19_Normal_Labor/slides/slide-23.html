Key Takeaways

Normal labor is spontaneous vertex delivery of a single full-term viable fetus without complications.
Four stages: cervical dilatation → fetal expulsion → placental delivery → observation.
Active management reduces 3rd stage duration from 15 min to 5 min, lowering PPH risk.
Partogram is essential for monitoring labor progress and detecting deviations early.
Friedman curve describes sigmoid pattern of cervical dilatation in the active phase.
Mechanism of labor involves 7 cardinal movements: descent, engagement, flexion, internal rotation, extension, restitution, external rotation.
