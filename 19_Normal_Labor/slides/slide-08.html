Four Stages of Labor — Overview

First Stage
Onset of true labor pain → full dilatation of cervix (10 cm)
Duration: 12 hrs (primi) / 6 hrs (multipara)
Second Stage
Full dilatation → expulsion of fetus
Duration: 1-2 hrs (primi) / 30 min (multipara)
Third Stage
Expulsion of fetus → expulsion of placenta & membranes
Duration: ~15 min (expectant) / 5 min (active management)
Fourth Stage
1 hour observation period after delivery of placenta. Critical for monitoring PPH.
