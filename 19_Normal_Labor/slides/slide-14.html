Abnormalities of Active Phase

Protracted Active Phase (slow cervical dilatation or descent)

Feature Nulliparous Multiparous Management
Cervical dilatation< 1.2 cm/hr< 1.5 cm/hrExpectant & support. If CPD → CS
Descent of head< 1 cm/hr< 2 cm/hr
Arrest Disorders
Arrest of Dilatation
Cessation of dilatation for 2 or more hours.
Arrest of Descent
Cessation of descent for 1 or more hours.
Factors contributing: Excessive sedation, Epidural analgesia, Fetal malposition, CPD.
Treatment: Expectant management and oxytocin in absence of any cephalopelvic disproportion.
