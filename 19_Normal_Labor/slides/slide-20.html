Active Management: Procedures (1st & 2nd Stage)

1st Stage Procedures
  • Evacuation of the rectum and bladder
  • Analgesia
  • Partogram: graphic recording of all events of labor — maternal (vital signs, uterine contractions, cervical dilatation, fluids and drugs) or fetal (fetal heart rate, head descent)
  • Amniotomy
  • Oxytocin to augment labor and regulate uterine contraction (3 in 10 minutes)
  • Fetal monitoring
2nd Stage Procedures
  • Transfer to delivery room
  • Delivery of the head: important goal is prevention of perineal lacerations by good support of the perineum and episiotomy
  • Clamping of the cord: contraindicated in preterm baby and in Rh – ve mothers
