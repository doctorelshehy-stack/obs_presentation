First Stage: Latent & Active Phases

Latent Phase
Starts from true labor pains → cervix 3-5 cm dilated.
Duration: 12 hrs (nulliparous) / 8 hrs (multiparous).
Mainly concerned with cervical effacement.
Active Phase
Begins with cervical dilatation of 3-5 cm with regular uterine contractions.
Normal dilatation rate: 1.2 cm/hr (primi) / 1.5 cm/hr (multiparous).
Mainly concerned with cervical dilatation.
