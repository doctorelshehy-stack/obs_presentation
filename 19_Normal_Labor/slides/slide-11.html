Physiology of the First Stage

Presentation
The first part of the fetus felt by vaginal examination.
Cephalic: 95% | Breech: 3.5% | Shoulder: 0.5%
Lie
Relation of long axis of fetus to mother.
Longitudinal: 99% | Transverse/Oblique: 1%
Attitude
Relation of fetal parts to each other.
Normally: generalized flexion
Position
Relation of denominator to mother's back.
OA more common than OP. Left OA > Right OA.
Denominator by Presentation
Vertex: Occiput
Face: Chin
Brow: Frontal bone
Breech: Sacrum
