Prelabor

It is the premonitory stage of labor and begins 2-3 weeks before the onset of true labor in primigravida and a few days before in multipara.

It is associated with an increase in oxytocin receptors in myometrium.

Lightening
Decrease in fundal height seen at term. Due to the descend of presenting part into the pelvis.
Cervical Ripening
Softening of the cervix in preparation for labor.
False Labor Pains
Irregular contractions that do not lead to cervical dilatation.
