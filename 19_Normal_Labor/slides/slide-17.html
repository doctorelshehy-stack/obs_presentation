Third Stage: Expectant vs Active Management

Extends from delivery of baby to complete expulsion of placenta and membranes.

Expectant Management
  • Placental separation and descent allowed to occur spontaneously with minimal assistance.
  • Duration: 15 mins
  • Increased chances of PPH and increased maternal mortality.
Active Management
  • Placental separation is actively facilitated and powerful uterine contractions initiated.
  • Duration reduced to 5 mins
  • Less chances of PPH and maternal mortality.
Active Management Includes:
Uterotonic soon after birth
Delayed cord clamping
Controlled cord traction
Uterine massage
