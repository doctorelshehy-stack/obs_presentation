Second Stage of Labor

Begins from full dilatation of cervix and ends with expulsion of fetus.

In the second stage, the expulsive efforts by a woman play more important role than uterine contractions.

2nd Stage Arrest
When there is no change in descent of head for 1 hour more than prolonged.
