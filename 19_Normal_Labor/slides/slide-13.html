Abnormalities of Latent Phase

Prolonged Latent Phase

  • Greater than 20 hours in primi
  • Greater than 14 hours in multipara
Causes
Excessive sedation or epidural analgesia.
Poor cervical conditions (thick, uneffaced or undilated).
False labor (most common cause in multipara).
Management
Therapeutic Test
15 mg morphine IM. Patient asleep within 1 hr, awake 4-5 hrs later — either in active labor or in no labor.
Oxytocin Stimulation
Oxytocin administration to stimulate regular contractions and progress labor.
