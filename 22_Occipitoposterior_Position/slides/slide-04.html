Definition & General Causes

Definition: It is vertex presentation in which the fetal back is directed posteriorly. It is the most common fetal malposition.
Fetal Causes
Marked deflexion of fetal head
Prematurity
Macrosomia
Multiple pregnancy
Polyhydramnios
Congenital anomalies (anencephaly, hydrocephalus)
Cord around the neck
Maternal Causes
Shape of pelvic inlet (Android, Anthropoid)
Pendulus abdomen
Placenta previa
Pelvic tumors
Uterine anomalies (bicornuate, septate)
Abnormal uterine contractions
