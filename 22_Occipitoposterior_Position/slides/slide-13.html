Management — Second Stage, Early CS & Arrested OP

Wait for Descent & Rotation
Wait for descent and long anterior rotation to allow normal vaginal delivery.
In Direct OP
Episiotomy is done to avoid perineal laceration.
Early CS Indications
Prolonged labor
Poor progress of labor
No rotation occurred
Abnormal uterine contractions
Fetal distress
Management of Arrested OP Position

Direct OP position and deep transverse arrest:

  • CS is preferred if the head is not engaged
  • Vacuum or forceps if the head is engaged
