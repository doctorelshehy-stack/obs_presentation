Definition

Decrease in total RBCs or hemoglobin in blood during pregnancy leading to decreased oxygen carrying capacity.

Normal Values During Pregnancy

1st Trimester
>11 g/dL
2nd Trimester
>10.5 g/dL
3rd Trimester
>11 g/dL
Postpartum
>10 g/dL
