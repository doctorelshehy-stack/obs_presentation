Pelvimetry — Clinical (Internal & External)

I. Clinical Pelvimetry — Internal (After 36 wks)

  • Diagonal conjugate: Tip of middle finger reaching sacral promontory. True conjugate = diagonal conjugate - 1.5 cm
  • AP diameter of pelvic outlet: As diagonal conjugate but tip touches tip of sacrum
  • Palpate shape of sacrum
  • Ischial spines: Not prominent, distance > 10.5 cm
  • Coccyx: Normally mobile, can recede backwards easily
  • Sacrospinous ligaments: Normally 2 fingers breadth (3.5 cm)
  • Side walls of pelvis: Normally parallel or divergent

External Pelvimetry of the Outlet

  • Subpubic angle (normally ≥ 90°): Can accommodate 2 fingers near apex
  • Bituberous diameter (normally 11 cm): Accommodates closed fist or knuckles of 4 fingers
  • AP diameter of outlet (normally 13 cm): Measured by 2 fingers in vagina

II. Imaging Pelvimetry

  • X-ray
  • Computerised Tomography (CT)
  • Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)
