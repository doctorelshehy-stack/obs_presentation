Precipitate Labor

Definition

A labor lasting less than 3 hours.

Etiology

More common in multiparas with: Strong uterine contractions + Small sized baby + Roomy pelvis.

Maternal Complications

  • Lacerations: cervix, vagina, perineum
  • Placental abruption
  • Shock
  • Inversion of the uterus
  • Postpartum hemorrhage (no time for retraction)
  • Sepsis (lacerations & unsuitable circumstances)

Fetal Complications

  • Intracranial hemorrhage (sudden compression/decompression)
  • Fetal asphyxia (reduced placental perfusion)
  • Avulsion of the umbilical cord
  • Fetal injury due to falling down

Management

Before Delivery

Hospitalize patient with previous precipitate labor before EDD.

During Delivery

Tocolytics (B agonists), inhalation anesthesia (N₂O + O₂), episiotomy.

After Delivery

Proper examination of genital system and fetus for injuries.
