Obstructed Labor — Diagnosis

General Examination

  • Labor prolonged (> 12 hours)
  • Mother appears exhausted, anxious and weak
  • Features of maternal distress: dehydration, exhaustion, ketoacidosis, tachypnea, scanty urine, raised temperature

Abdominal Examination

  • Uterus: hard and tender
  • Pathological retraction ring
  • Distended bladder
  • Fetal heart: distress or absent

Vaginal Examination

  • Vulva: Edematous
  • Vagina: Dry and hot
  • Cervix: Fully or partially dilated, edematous
  • Membranes: Ruptured
  • Presenting part: High, not engaged or impacted. Large caput and moulding
  • Cause of obstruction can be detected
