Obstructed Labor — Management & Complications

Management

Prevention

  • Proper assessment with regular ANC visits
  • Early detection of maternal or fetal causes
  • Partogram should be strictly followed

General Management

  • Assessment of general condition
  • Correction of dehydration with IV fluids
  • Broad spectrum antibiotics
  • Catheterization
  • Correction of acidosis

Obstetric Management

  • Cesarean section with active management of 3rd stage (fear of PPH)
  • Exploration of the birth canal

Complications

Maternal

  • Postpartum hemorrhage
  • Rupture uterus
  • Sepsis
  • Fistula (vesicovaginal)

Fetal

  • Neonatal sepsis
  • Convulsions (fits)
  • Severe asphyxia
  • Death
