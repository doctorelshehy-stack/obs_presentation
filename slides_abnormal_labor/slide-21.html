Contracted Pelvis — Complications & Management

Maternal Complications

  • Pendulous abdomen
  • Nonengagement
  • Pyelonephritis (high assimilation pelvis → ureter compression)
  • Inertia, slow cervical dilatation, prolonged labor
  • PROM and cord prolapse
  • Obstructed labor and rupture uterus
  • Necrotic genito-urinary fistula
  • Injury to pelvic joints/nerves from difficult instrumental delivery
  • Postpartum hemorrhage

Fetal Complications

  • Intracranial hemorrhage
  • Birth asphyxia
  • Fracture skull
  • Nerve injuries
  • Intra-amniotic infection

Management

  • Minor degree: Vaginal delivery recommended
  • Moderate degree: Trial of labor, if failed → CS
  • Severe or extreme degree: Cesarean section required

Indications of Cesarean Section

  • Moderate disproportion if trial of labor contraindicated/failed
  • Extreme disproportion (living or dead fetus)
  • Contracted outlet
  • Contracted pelvis with other indications: elderly primigravida, malpresentations, placenta previa
