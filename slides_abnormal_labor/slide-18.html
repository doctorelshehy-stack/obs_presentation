Classification of Contracted Pelvis

A) Pelvic Inlet Contraction

Definition

  • Shortest AP diameter < 10 cm
  • Greatest transverse diameter < 12 cm
  • Diagonal conjugate < 11.5 cm

Effects

  • Non-engagement in primigravida last 2 wks
  • OP, face and shoulder presentation (3x)
  • Cord prolapse (4-6x more frequent)
  • PROM

B) Midpelvic Contraction

Definition

  • Interspinous + posterior sagittal < 13.5 cm

Clinical Diagnosis

  • Ischial spines prominent
  • Side walls convergent
  • Sacrosciatic notch < 2 fingers

Effect

Transverse arrest of head → ↑ CS rate

C) Pelvic Outlet Contraction

Definition (1%)

  • Bituberous diameter < 8 cm
  • Narrow subpubic angle (normal 90-100°)

Clinical Diagnosis

  • Subpubic angle doesn't admit 2 fingers
  • Bituberos doesn't admit 4 knuckles
  • AP diameter of outlet < 13 cm

Effect

↑ Perineal lacerations, dystocia (midpelvic)

D) Both Inlet & Outlet: Generally Contracted Pelvis

Classification by Degree of Contraction (4 Degrees)

1st (Minor)

TC 9-10 cm

Spontaneous delivery possible

2nd (Moderate)

TC 8-9 cm

Spontaneous possible but complications

3rd (Severe)

TC 6-8 cm

Spontaneous impossible → CS

4th (Extreme)

TC < 6 cm

Absolutely contracted → CS
