Summary

Abnormal Uterine Action

Inefficient contractions (3 Ps: Power)
Coordinate: Inertia, Precipitate, Bandl's
Incoordinate: Constriction ring, Colicky, Cervical dystocia
Management: Amniotomy, Oxytocin, CS

Obstructed Labor

Mechanical obstruction + good contractions
Maternal: CPD, tumors, stenosis
Fetal: Macrosomia, malpresentation, malformed
Management: CS + active 3rd stage

Contracted Pelvis

Inlet/Midpelvic/Outlet contraction
4 degrees: Minor→spontaneous, Moderate→trial, Severe/Extreme→CS
Pinard & Muller-Kerr CPD tests
Pelvimetry: Clinical & Imaging
