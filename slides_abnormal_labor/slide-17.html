Contracted Pelvis — Definition & Etiology

Anatomical Definition

A pelvis in which one or more of its diameters is reduced below normal by one or more centimeters.

Obstetric Definition

A pelvis that is abnormally small in one or more of its principal diameters that interferes with normal childbirth and the normal mechanism of labor.

Etiology

A) Congenital

  • Naegele's pelvis: Asymmetric/obliquely CP due to absence of one sacral ala
  • Robert's pelvis: Transversely CP due to absence of 2 sacral alae (double Naegele)
  • High assimilation pelvis: Sacrum of 6 fused segments
  • Low assimilation pelvis: Sacrum of 4 fused segments

B) Acquired

Pelvic bones:

  • Fractures/tumors of pelvic bones
  • Metabolic: Rickets or osteomalacia

Vertebral column:

  • Kyphosis, Scoliosis, Spondylolisthesis

Lower limb:

  • Missing/shortening of limb (childhood/adolescence)
  • Fractures, Inflammatory (TB osteomyelitis)
