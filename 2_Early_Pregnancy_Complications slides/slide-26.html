Etiology / Risk Factors

  • Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID)
  • Previous ectopic
  • Previous tubal surgery
  • Previous pelvic or abdominal surgery
  • Tubal pathology
  • IUCD (Intrauterine Contraceptive Device)
  • Tubal sterilization
  • Cigarette smoking

Tubal Implantation Sites

• Ampulla: 80%

• Isthmus: 12%

• Interstitial part

• Fimbrial end
