Mechanism of Labor in Breech (Sacro-Anterior)

A) Buttocks

  1. Descent of the buttocks
  2. Engagement: by the bi-trochanteric diameter (10 cm) in the oblique diameters (12 cm) of the pelvis
  3. Internal rotation: Anterior buttock rotates 1/8th of a circle so bitrochanteric diameter lies in AP diameter of pelvic outlet
  4. The anterior buttock hinges under the symphysis pubis
  5. By lateral flexion of fetal spine over symphysis pubis, the posterior buttock is delivered
  6. By straightening of the spine, the anterior buttock is delivered
  7. External rotation occurs so that the sacrum lies anterior

B) Shoulders

  1. The shoulders engage by the bisacromial diameter (11.8 cm) in the same oblique diameter as the buttock
  2. Internal rotation: Anterior shoulder rotates 1/8 of a circle anteriorly (levator ani contraction), bisacromial diameter lies in AP diameter of pelvic outlet
  3. The anterior shoulder hinges under symphysis pubis
  4. By lateral flexion of spine, posterior shoulder is delivered
  5. By straightening of spine, anterior shoulder is delivered
