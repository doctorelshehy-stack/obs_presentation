Shoulder Presentation — Definition & Etiology

Definition

A malpresentation in which the fetus is in a transverse lie (its vertebral column is perpendicular to that of the mother).

Etiology

  • Grand multiparity (5 deliveries or more), lax abdominal musculature
  • Uterine abnormalities (malformations or fibroids)
  • Twin pregnancy
  • Uterine overdistension (polyhydramnios)
  • Prematurity and small fetus
  • Fetal malformation
  • Placenta previa
  • Feto-pelvic disproportion

Positions

  • Denominator: The scapula (Sc)
  • 4 positions: LScA, RScA, LScP, RScP
  • Commonest position: LScA
