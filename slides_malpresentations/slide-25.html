Complicated Breech Delivery

A) Arrest of Buttocks at Pelvic Inlet

Causes & Management

  • Uterine inertia → Oxytocin drip
  • Contracted pelvis → Cesarean section
  • Large fetus → Cesarean section

B) Arrest of Buttocks at Pelvic Outlet (Impacted Breech)

Causes & Management

  • Uterine inertia → Groin traction + oxytocin, if fails → Bring down legs
  • Rigid perineum → Episiotomy
  • Pelvic outlet contraction → Cesarean section
  • Large fetus → Cesarean section
  • Frank Breech (extension of legs): Deeply impacted → groin traction. Not deeply impacted → Bring down legs

Pinard Method (Bringing Down a Leg)

Under GA, empty bladder, episiotomy. Pass hand along anterior thigh to popliteal fossa. Press with 2 fingers to flex leg, grasp foot and bring down. Breech extraction then performed. Bring anterior leg first (bringing posterior leg first causes obstruction).

Groin Traction

Used when breech deeply impacted. Index finger in anterior groin for traction during contraction. Traction downward & backward until anterior buttock appears below symphysis, then upward for posterior buttock. Traction towards trunk, NOT thigh (avoid femur fracture). Breech hook may be used if fetus dead.
