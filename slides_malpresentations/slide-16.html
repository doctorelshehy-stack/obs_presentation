Methods for Delivering the Aftercoming Head

Mauriceau-Smellie-Veit (MSV)

  • Two fingers of left hand on maxilla to avoid jaw dislocation
  • Index & ring finger of right hand on each shoulder
  • Middle finger pressing against occiput to promote flexion
  • Splints neck, prevents hyperextension → cervical spine injury
  • Traction downwards & backwards until nape appears, then lift body towards mother's abdomen

Burns-Marshall Method

  • Let the baby hang down by its own weight
  • Suprapubic pressure given by flat hand in downward & backward direction
  • As nape of neck visible, grasp the feet and lift body towards mother's abdomen

Piper's Forceps Delivery

Piper's forceps are specially designed forceps for delivery of the aftercoming head in breech presentation.

Advantages of Piper's Forceps

  • Protect the head from sudden compression-decompression and intracranial hemorrhage
  • Minimize traction on the fetal neck
  • Maintain flexion of the head
  • Decrease incidence of perineal tears
