Management of Breech Presentation

During Pregnancy

External Cephalic Version (ECV) — manipulation of the fetus to a cephalic presentation through the maternal abdomen aiming for vaginal delivery.

During Labor

First stage: Management similar to other malpresentations.

External Cephalic Version (ECV)

Timing

  • ECV should be offered from 37 weeks' gestation
  • In primiparous women, ECV can be offered from 36 weeks' gestation
