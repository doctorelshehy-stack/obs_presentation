Compound Presentation — Diagnosis & Management

Diagnosis

  • Delayed engagement of the presenting part
  • Per Vaginal (PV) examination: The prolapsed limb is felt beside the presenting part

Management

  • First attempt: Gently try to reduce the prolapsed limb upwards above the pelvic brim and hold it till a contraction, then push the head down into the pelvis
  • Cesarean Section is indicated if:
    • Abnormal FHR pattern
    • Cord prolapse
    • Poor progress of labor

Student Activity: Each group to prepare a diagram illustrative of compound presentation.
