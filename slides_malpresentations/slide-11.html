Breech Presentation — Definition, Incidence & Etiology

Definition

Fetus in a longitudinal lie in which the presenting part is the buttocks or feet (podalic part).

Incidence

3-4% of all deliveries

Etiology — Accommodation Theory

The fetus is usually adapted to the pyriform shape of the uterus with the large buttock in the fundus and the smaller head in the lower uterine segment.

Predisposing Factors

  • Prematurity
  • Uterine malformations or fibroids
  • Polyhydramnios
  • Placenta previa
  • Fetal abnormalities (CNS malformations, neck masses, aneuploidy)
  • Multiple gestations
  • Cord around the neck
  • Pelvic tumors
