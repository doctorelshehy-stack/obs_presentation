Summary of Malpresentations

Brow Presentation

Midway flexion/extension
Mentovertical 13.75cm → obstructed
Management: Cesarean Section

Compound Presentation

Limb prolapsed beside head
Reduce limb or CS if FHR abnormal / cord prolapse / poor progress

Breech Presentation

3-4% deliveries. Frank/Complete/Footling
ECV at 36-37 wks, assisted breech delivery or CS
Head most difficult part — MSV, Burns-Marshall, Piper's forceps

Shoulder Presentation

Transverse lie, perpendicular spine
ECV at 36 wks → CS if fails
Neglected shoulder: impacted + prolapsed arm → CS

Cord Prolapse

Cord below presenting part after ROM
Living fetus: immediate delivery (CS/forceps)
Dead fetus: spontaneous delivery

Shoulder Dystocia

Turtle sign — head retracts
McRobert's first (90% resolve)
HELPERR sequence → Zavanelli
