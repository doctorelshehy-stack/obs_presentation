Arrest of the Aftercoming Head

Causes and Management

Contracted Pelvis & Large Head

  • If fetus alive → Symphysiotomy
  • If fetus dead → Craniotomy

Extension of the Head

  • Burns-Marshall technique
  • Jaw flexion & shoulder traction
  • Application of the forceps

Rigid Perineum

→ Episiotomy

Incompletely Dilated Cervix

→ Dührssen Incisions made at 2, 6 & 10 o'clock positions on the cervix
