Neglected Shoulder & Internal Podalic Version

Neglected Shoulder Presentation

Definition

Obstructed labor in which the fetal shoulder is impacted with prolapsed arm, drained amniotic fluid, and the fetus is severely distressed or dead.

Management

Cesarean section

Internal Podalic Version

Definition

Manual intrauterine procedure to convert a cephalic presentation or a transverse lie into a breech to allow a total breech extraction.

Indications

  • Delivery of a second twin in cephalic presentation or transverse lie

Precautions and Technique

  • Strict asepsis: swab perineum with 10% povidone iodine, sterile gloves
  • Spinal anesthesia if possible
  • Insert right hand, determine fetal position, hold fundus with other hand
  • Grasp one foot (or both if possible) firmly. Better not to rupture membranes immediately (uterine retraction and lack of amniotic fluid makes grasping difficult)
  • Pull foot/feet gently to the vaginal opening
  • Delivery continues as breech delivery
  • Manually explore uterus after placenta delivery for uterine rupture
  • Administer routine antibiotic prophylaxis
