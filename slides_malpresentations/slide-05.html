Brow Presentation — Diagnosis

During Pregnancy

  • The occiput and sinciput are felt at the same level on abdominal palpation
  • Ultrasound is conclusive in confirming diagnosis

During Labor (Per Vaginal Examination)

  • The frontal bone is felt
  • The supraorbital ridge is felt
  • The root of the nose is felt
  • The chin is NOT felt (key distinguishing feature from face presentation)
