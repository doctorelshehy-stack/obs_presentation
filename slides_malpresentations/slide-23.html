Total Breech Extraction

Definition

The obstetrician carries out the entire delivery of the baby.

Indications

  • Delivery of the second twin (main indication nowadays)
  • Maternal or fetal distress (e.g., prolapsed pulsating cord + fully dilated cervix)
  • Uterine inertia with prolonged second stage of labor

Prerequisite

The cervix must be fully dilated before proceeding.

Technique

  • Delivery of buttocks: General anesthesia + generous episiotomy. Dislodge breech upwards, bring down one leg then the other, direct back forwards, traction on legs and fundal pressure helps delivery of trunk up to umbilicus
  • Delivery of shoulders & aftercoming head: Same as assisted breech delivery
