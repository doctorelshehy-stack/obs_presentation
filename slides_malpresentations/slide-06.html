Brow Presentation — Mechanism, Management & Complications

Mechanism of Labor

The engaged diameter is the mentovertical diameter (13.75 cm), which is longer than any diameter of the female pelvic inlet. Therefore, labor becomes obstructed from the start.

Management

Cesarean Section

Vaginal delivery is not possible due to obstructed labor.

Complications

Maternal

  • PROM (Premature Rupture of Membranes)
  • Cord prolapse

Fetal

  • Related to operative delivery
  • Related to obstructed labor
