Shoulder Dystocia — Management (Steps 6-10)

Step 6: Rubin Maneuver

The anterior fetal shoulder is rotated obliquely with a vaginal hand.

Step 7: Wood Corkscrew

The posterior shoulder is rotated over 180 degrees with a vaginal hand to assist delivery of the shoulders.

Step 8: Gaskin Maneuver

Facilitated in a patient without regional anesthesia. Turn the patient over on "all fours" position, inverting the anterior and posterior shoulders.

Step 9: Cleidotomy

Cutting of the clavicle to reduce the bisacromial diameter.

Step 10: Symphysiotomy

Incising the cartilaginous portion of the symphysis pubis to release the anterior shoulder.

Step 11: Zavanelli Maneuver

The fetal head is flexed and pushed back up into the uterus, followed by emergent cesarean section.

Sequence Summary (HELPERR Mnemonic)

Help → Episiotomy → McRobert's → Suprapubic Pressure → Posterior Arm → Rubin → Wood Corkscrew → Gaskin → Cleidotomy → Symphysiotomy → Zavanelli
