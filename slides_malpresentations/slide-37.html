Shoulder Dystocia — Management (Steps 1-5)

Step 1: Immediately after recognition, call extra help — Senior obstetrician, pediatric resuscitation team, anesthetist.

Step 2: Episiotomy

Performed to facilitate different maneuvers.

Step 3: McRobert's Maneuver

Flexion and abduction of maternal hips, positioning the maternal thighs on her abdomen. Most effective intervention — perform first! Resolves 90% of cases. If delivery not achieved, add suprapubic pressure.

Step 4: Suprapubic Pressure

Applied in conjunction with McRobert's maneuver to dislodge the impacted anterior shoulder.

Step 5: Delivery of Posterior Arm

  • Insert a hand in the vagina and flex the elbow
  • Sweep arm over the chest
  • Deliver the posterior arm

This reduces the bisacromial diameter and allows the anterior shoulder to disimpact.
