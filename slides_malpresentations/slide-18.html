ECV — Contraindications, Complications & Technique

Contraindications

  • Ruptured membranes
  • Severe pre-eclampsia
  • Abnormal CTG or Doppler
  • Lack of maternal consent
  • Absolute indication for CS
  • Multiple pregnancy (except after first twin delivery)
  • Scarred uterus
  • Placenta previa / recent vaginal bleeding
  • Rhesus isoimmunization
  • No access to US / emergency CS / fetal monitoring

Complications

  • Fetal heart abnormalities (e.g., bradycardia)
  • Placental abruption
  • Uterine rupture
  • Preterm labor
  • PPROM and cord prolapse

Precautions and Technique

  • Informed written consent from patient
  • Ultrasound to verify presentation & abnormalities
  • CTG prior to procedure
  • Use tocolysis
  • Administer Anti-D if Rh-negative
  • Maximum 4 attempts, suggested max 10 minutes total
  • CTG monitoring post-procedure
