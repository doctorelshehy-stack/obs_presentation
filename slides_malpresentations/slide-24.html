Indications of Cesarean Section in Breech Presentation

Absolute Indications

  1. Breech in primigravida
  2. Premature, viable infant
  3. Footling presentation
  4. Hyperextension of fetal head (vaginal delivery → cervical spine injury)
  5. Estimated fetal weight by US > 3800 gm
  6. Contracted pelvis
  7. History of difficult delivery
  8. Chronic fetal distress (IUGR, DM, hypertensive disorders)
  9. General indications of CS
  10. Prolapsed pulsating cord with incompletely dilated cervix
