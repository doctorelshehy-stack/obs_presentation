Mechanism of Labor — C) Head

Mechanism of the Aftercoming Head

  1. The head engages by the occipito-frontal diameter (11.5 cm) in the opposite oblique diameter of the pelvic inlet (opposite to buttock & shoulder)
  2. Internal rotation: The occiput rotates 1/8 of a circle in sacro-anterior position and 3/8 of a circle in sacro-posterior position
  3. The suboccipital region reaches the symphysis pubis
  4. The head is then delivered by flexion

Important Note

The head is the most difficult part to deliver per vaginally in breech presentation. Various methods exist to deliver the aftercoming head.
