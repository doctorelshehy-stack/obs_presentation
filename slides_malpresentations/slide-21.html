Assisted Breech Delivery — Sequential Maneuvers

1. Initial Delivery & Traction

With a towel wrapped around fetal hips, gentle downward and outward traction is applied with maternal expulsive efforts until the scapula is reached. An assistant applies gentle fundal pressure to keep the head flexed.

2. Arm Delivery — Løvset Maneuver

Once the scapula is visible, rotate the infant 90° and gently sweep the anterior arm out of the vagina by pressing on the inner aspect of the arm or elbow. Then rotate the infant 180° in the reverse direction, and sweep the other arm out. Finally rotate back 90° so the back is anterior.

3. Head Delivery

The fetal head should be maintained in a flexed position to allow passage of the smallest diameter. This can be accomplished by:

  • Mauriceau-Smellie-Veit maneuver: Operator's index and middle fingers over the maxilla, assistant applies suprapubic pressure
  • Piper forceps: Used to maintain the head in a flexed position

⚠ Important: Avoid extreme elevation of the body during head delivery, which may cause hyperextension of the cervical spine and potential neurologic injury.
