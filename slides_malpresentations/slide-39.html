Shoulder Dystocia — Complications

Fetal Complications

  • Brachial plexus injury
  • Erb's palsy
  • Bone fractures (humerus and clavicle)
  • Hypoxic ischemic brain injury
  • Fetal death

Maternal Complications

  • Post-partum hemorrhage
  • Vaginal lacerations and anal tears
  • Uterine rupture
  • Maternal psychological stress

ILOs of Shoulder Dystocia Chapter

  • Understand definition of shoulder dystocia
  • Outline the risk factors of such obstetric emergency
  • Describe the clinical picture and management of shoulder dystocia
  • Explain possible maternal and fetal complications
