Cord Presentation & Prolapse — Definitions & Etiology

Cord Presentation

Presence of the umbilical cord below the fetal presenting part which can be felt through intact membranes.

Cord Prolapse

Presence of coils of the umbilical cord below the presenting part after rupture of the membranes.

Etiology / Risk Factors

  • Prematurity
  • Fetal congenital abnormality
  • Second twin
  • Multiparity
  • Low birth weight (< 2.5 kg)
  • Breech, oblique, transverse and unstable lie
  • Cephalopelvic disproportion
  • Low-lying placenta
  • Long umbilical cord
  • Polyhydramnios
  • Macrosomia
