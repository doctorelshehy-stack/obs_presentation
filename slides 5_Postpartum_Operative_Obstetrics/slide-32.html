Puerperal Sepsis — Sites of Infection & Pathology

Pathology

I. Primary Sites

  1. Lacerations of the vulva, vagina or cervix.
  2. Uterus: placental site, retained placental tissue or membranes.

II. Secondary Sites

Usually occur as extension from primary sites via:

  1. Pelvic spread: Acute PID, Parametritis, Pelvic abscess.
  2. Blood spread: May lead to bacteremia or septicemia.
