Forceps Delivery

Operative Obstetrics

Definition

A double-bladed metal instrument used for fetal head extraction.

Indications

  • A) Prolonged second stage.
  • B) To shorten the second stage.
  • C) Fetal: fetal distress, prolapsed pulsating cord, preterm delivery, after-coming head in breech delivery.

Types of Forceps

  • Long curved obstetric forceps — standard design
  • Wrigley / Simpson forceps — shorter blades
  • Kielland’s forceps — for deep transverse arrest of fetal head
  • Piper forceps — long perineal curve for after-coming head in breech delivery

Conditions Before Application

  • Anesthesia
  • Adequate pelvic outlet
  • Aseptic measures
  • Bladder and bowel evacuation
  • Contractions of the uterus
  • Fully dilated cervix
  • Engaged head and favorable positions
  • Ruptured membranes

Complications

  • Complications of anesthesia
  • Lacerations
  • Bone injuries and pelvic nerves injury
  • Postpartum hemorrhage
  • Puerperal infections, genital prolapse, VVF
  • Fracture fetal skull and cephalohematoma
  • Fetal intracranial hemorrhage and facial nerve palsy
  • Trauma to fetal face or eyes and asphyxia
