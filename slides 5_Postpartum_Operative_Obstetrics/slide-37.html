Puerperal Sepsis — Surgical Treatment & Student Activity

Management & Review

Incision & Drainage of Abscess

  • Pelvic abscess: Posterior colpotomy + drain.
  • Parametric abscess: Incision + drain at the pointing point.

Other Treatments

  • Treatment of complications (DIC, renal failure).
  • Treatment of thrombophlebitis: anticoagulants.

Student Activity

Each student should attend with the tutor of bedside part of the clinical round the postpartum care phase of a delivered woman and notice measures for prevention of puerperal sepsis.
