Uterine Inversion — Risk Factors & Degrees

Complications of Third Stage of Labor

Risk Factors

  • Rapid labor
  • Invasive placentation
  • Manual removal of placenta
  • Short umbilical cord

Degrees of Uterine Inversion

  • 1st degree (incomplete): The fundus is depressed within the endometrial cavity.
  • 2nd degree (complete): The fundus protrudes through the cervical os.
  • 3rd degree (prolapsed): The fundus protrudes to or beyond the introitus.

Symptoms

  • Pain: May be felt in the lower abdomen with bearing down.
  • Hemorrhage: Due to uterine atony.

Signs

  • General: Variable degrees of shock (hemorrhagic or neurogenic)
  • Abdominal: Incomplete: cupping of fundus; Complete: uterus not felt
  • Vaginal: Large mass in vagina covered by dark red endometrium
