Episiotomy

Operative Obstetrics

Definition

An incision into the perineal body to enlarge the outlet area and facilitate delivery.

Types

  • Median type: Easier to repair but possible risk of unintended extension to anal sphincter complex.
  • Mediolateral type: Requires more skill for repair.

Anesthesia

Local infiltration anesthesia.

Indications

  1. When there is impending perineal tear.
  2. Vaginal delivery after complete perineal tear repair.
  3. In cases of forceps or ventouse delivery, breech and preterm delivery.

Episiotomy Repair

Three parts: vaginal mucosal, perineal part, and skin of perineal part. Vaginal part repaired by continuous absorbable sutures. Perineal part by continuous or intermittent sutures. Skin closed by subcutaneous absorbable sutures.

Complications

  • Vulvar hematoma (due to lack of good surgical hemostasis)
  • Infection of episiotomy wound
  • Rectovaginal fistula (bad surgical repair)
  • Allergic reaction to local infiltration anesthesia
