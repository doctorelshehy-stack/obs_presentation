CS — Subsequent Deliveries & Special Procedures

Operative Obstetrics

VBAC (Vaginal Birth after CS)

  • The dictum “once cesarean always cesarean” is changed to “once lower segment cesarean always hospital delivery (in a well-equipped hospital).”
  • Repeated CS if indicated.
  • If no indication for repeated CS, a trial of vaginal delivery is allowed in hospital with careful observation (VBAC).
  • Applies only for lower segment CS. No vaginal trial for upper segment CS (higher risk of ruptured uterus: 4% or more).

Indications of Cesarean Hysterectomy

  • Uncontrolled postpartum hemorrhage
  • Concealed accidental hemorrhage (uterus doesn’t respond to usual measures)
  • Operable carcinoma of the cervix
  • Placenta accreta
  • Multiple fibroids in a relatively old patient

Postmortem CS

Emergency CS within 10 minutes of the death of the mother to save the living fetus.

Sterilization during CS

Patient may ask for sterilization during 3rd or 4th CS. Performed by excision of a portion of both tubes and ligation, or bilateral excision of interstitial portion of fallopian tube.
