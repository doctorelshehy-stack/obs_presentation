Version

Operative Obstetrics

Definition

Turning of fetus from one presentation to another, done either externally or internally by the physician. If the aim is to make the head the presenting part: cephalic version. If breech will be the presenting part: podalic version.

Types

  • External cephalic version
  • Internal cephalic version
  • Bipolar version

External Cephalic Version (No anesthesia)

Done antenatally after 36 weeks to turn fetus from breech/transverse to cephalic by external maneuvers.

Indications:

  • Breech presentation
  • Transverse lie or oblique lie

Internal Podalic Version (GA required)

Turning fetus inside the uterus through internal maneuvers to achieve breech presentation followed by breech extraction. The only remaining indication: transverse lie of a second co-twin after delivery of the first twin.

Bipolar Version (GA required)

Done for correction of transverse lie of a dead fetus. One or two fingers introduced through the cervix with the other hand on the abdomen. A fetal foot is grasped and pulled through the cervix while the other hand assists externally.
