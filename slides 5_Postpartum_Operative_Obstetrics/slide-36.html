Puerperal Sepsis — Treatment

Management

General Measures

  • Isolation in a separate room or fever hospital
  • Light diet rich in vitamins and minerals with plenty of fluids
  • Restoration of fluid/electrolyte balance; correction of anemia
  • Analgesics for pain; antipyretics and cold fomentations for fever
  • Observations: pulse, temperature, BP, vaginal bleeding, lochia, manifestations of DVT

Antibiotic Therapy

Broad spectrum antibiotic (ampicillin or cephalosporin) + gentamycin + metronidazole OR Clindamycin + gentamycin.

Antitoxin serum given in Cl. welchii infection.

Promotion of Drainage

  • Fowler’s or semisitting position
  • Removal of stitches if purulent discharge from wound
  • Misoprostol or ergometrine for drainage of lochia
