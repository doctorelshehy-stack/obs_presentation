Summary

This presentation covered the essential aspects of postpartum complications and operative obstetrics:

  • Uterine Inversion & Retained Placenta
  • Amniotic Fluid Embolism
  • Puerperal Sepsis
  • Cesarean Section, D&C, Forceps, Vacuum, Episiotomy, Version
  • Gynecological Instruments
