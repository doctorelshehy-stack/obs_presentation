PPH — Complications

Complications of Third Stage of Labor

Complications of Blood Loss

  • When the patient loses 20% of blood: tachycardia, tachypnea, narrowed pulse pressure, delayed capillary refill
  • Ischemic injury to the liver, brain, heart, and kidney
  • Sheehan syndrome (postpartum hypopituitarism)

Complications of Management

  • Transfusion-related acute lung injury
  • Infections
  • Hemolytic transfusion reactions
