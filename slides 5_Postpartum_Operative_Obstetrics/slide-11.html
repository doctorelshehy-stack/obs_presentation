Management — Uterotonic Agents (Medication)

Complications of Third Stage of Labor

Oxytocin

Effective first-line treatment. 10-40 international units (IU) IM, or IV drip.

Ergot Alkaloids (Methylergonovine / Ergometrine)

Cause generalized smooth muscle contraction; upper and lower segments contract tetanically. 0.2-0.4 mg IM, repeated as required every 2-4 hours. Contraindicated in women with preeclampsia or hypertension (raises BP). Side effects: nausea, vomiting.

Carboprost (15-methyl PGF2a)

Prostaglandin. Enhances uterine contractility and causes vasoconstriction. 0.25 mg intramyometrially or IM, repeated every 15 min for a total dose of 2 mg. Side effects: nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, hypertension, headache, flushing, pyrexia.

Misoprostol

Prostaglandin that increases uterine tone and decreases postpartum bleeding. Administered sublingually, orally, vaginally, rectally. Doses: 200-1000 mcg. Side effects: shivering, pyrexia, diarrhea.
