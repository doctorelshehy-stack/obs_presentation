Dilatation & Curettage (D&C)

Operative Obstetrics

Definition

Surgical procedure in which evacuation of uterine contents or products of conception is performed when indicated.

Steps

  1. Dorsal lithotomy position.
  2. General, regional or local anesthesia after Betadine disinfection of perineum and vagina.
  3. Sounding of uterus after introduction of self-retaining vaginal speculum.
  4. General gradual dilatation of cervix up to Hegar’s 10 or more.
  5. Ovum forceps introduced into uterus, opened to grasp contents, extracted while rotating.
  6. IV infusion of 10 IU oxytocin in 500cc glucose 5% or saline.
  7. Uterus curetted to remove any remaining products of conception fragment and decidua.

Causes of Bleeding During/After Evacuation

  • Remaining parts of products of conception
  • Trauma of the cervix or uterine perforation
  • Atony of the uterus

Treatment of Bleeding after D&C

  • Check for missed uterine contents by more curettage
  • Repair (suturing) of traumatized cervix
  • Ecbolics for uterine atony

Complications

  • Anesthetic complications
  • Uterine trauma (perforation, cervical tears)
  • Infection
