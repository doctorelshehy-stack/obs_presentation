Puerperal Sepsis — Diagnosis

Clinical Assessment

History & Symptoms

History of risk factors. Symptoms appear shortly (48-72 hours) following labor or abortion:

  1. Fever and rigors
  2. Abdominal pain and tenderness
  3. Offensive vaginal discharge
  4. Symptoms of complications

Examination

  • General: Temperature >38°C for 24 hours
  • Abdominal: Subinvoluted tender uterus
  • Local: Offensive vaginal discharge, secondary postpartum hemorrhage
  • Speculum Examination to inspect cervix and vagina
