Vacuum Extraction

Operative Obstetrics

Definition

Traction of fetal head using a cup applied near occiput by negative pressure.

Indications

The same as forceps.

Contraindications

  1. Severe cephalo-pelvic disproportion
  2. Other presentation than vertex
  3. Premature fetus
  4. Intact membranes

Complications

A) Fetal:

  1. Cephalohematoma
  2. Scalp laceration
  3. Rarely, intracranial hemorrhage

B) Maternal:

  1. Lacerations
  2. Annular detachment of cervix
