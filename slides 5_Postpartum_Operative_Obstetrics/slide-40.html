Cesarean Section — Definition & Incidence

Operative Obstetrics

Definition

Delivery of a viable fetus through an abdominal and uterine incision. It’s the most common major operation performed globally.

Incidence

Globally, in the last 35 years, the rate has steadily increased from 5% to 30%. Egypt has a higher incidence approaching 70%.

Indications

A) Fetal:

  • Non-reassuring FHR tracing
  • Non-vertex presentation
  • Fetal anomalies (hydrocephalus)
  • Umbilical cord prolapse
  • Conjoined twins

B) Maternal:

  • Obstruction of lower genital tract (e.g. large cervical fibroid)
  • Previous CS (if VBAC declined/not appropriate)
  • History of severe pelvic floor injury
  • Abdominal cerclage

C) Maternal & Fetal:

  • Abruptio placentae, Active HSV infection, Dystocia/CPD, Placenta previa/vasa previa
