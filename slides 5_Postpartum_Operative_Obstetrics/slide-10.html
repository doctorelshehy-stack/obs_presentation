Management of Atonic PPH — Initial Measures

Complications of Third Stage of Labor

IV Fluids & Blood Products

Intravenous (IV) fluids, blood, and blood products should be given rapidly to prevent shock.

Uterine Massage

A simple first-line treatment that helps the uterus contract to reduce bleeding.

Bimanual Compression

One hand in the vagina pushing against the body of the uterus while the other hand compresses the fundus from above through the abdominal wall. The posterior aspect of the uterus is massaged with the abdominal hand and the anterior aspect with the vaginal hand.

Manual Aortic Compression

If there is severe bleeding (within a few minutes), apply direct pressure to the aorta to compress it against the vertebrae a few centimeters superior to the sacral promontory using a closed fist.
